FEATURED LESSON

What is a computer network?

A computer network is a group of connected devices that communicate and share resources such as files, printers and Internet access.

  • Router: connects different networks.
  • Switch: connects devices within a local network.
  • IP address: identifies a device on a network.
  • Protocol: rules used for communication.
